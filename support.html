Support and help

Need help? Email comicbookhistorians@gmail.com and say which iPhone or iPad you have and what happened. Most messages get a reply within two days.

Common questions

The game is sideways or looks squished.
The game plays in landscape only. Turn the device sideways and make sure the rotation lock in Control Center is off. On an iPad a thin dark strip above and below the picture is normal.
I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on the device itself. Deleting the app deletes the save. To move progress to another device, open the Workshop from the episode map, tap Save code, tap Copy code, then paste it into Load code on the other device.
How do I start over?
Open the Workshop from the episode map and tap Erase progress. This cannot be undone.
There is no sound.
Check the mute switch on the side of the iPhone and the volume buttons. The game also has its own sound button in the top right corner of the screen.
Why does it ask for a birthdate?
Only to decide which terms screen to show. The date is not saved and is not sent anywhere.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The game is a one-time purchase with nothing else to buy, no ads, no accounts, and no internet connection needed.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the game, and choose Request a refund.
A level seems impossible.
Try a different trap mix: walls make heroes jump, arrows hit harder while they are in the air, and glue stops the jump. Stars earned on earlier levels unlock Workshop perks that make later levels easier. Difficulty can be lowered in the Workshop at any time.

Report a bug

Email the address above with the episode and level number, what you tapped, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
