Sir Ladderton and the Unfair Tower

Support, help and privacy

Need help? Email comicbookhistorians@gmail.com and say which iPhone or iPad you have and what happened. Most messages get a reply within two days.

Common questions

How do I play?
Tap anywhere to jump. Hold the tap a little longer for a higher jump. The LADDER button in the bottom right corner spends one ladder to cross a pit, climb a wall or prop up a crusher.
The game is sideways.
The game plays in landscape only. Turn the device sideways. On an iPad a thin dark strip above and below the picture is normal.
Where does my run get saved?
Every 10 traps the game saves a checkpoint on the device. The title screen starts from the latest checkpoint, and the plus and minus buttons pick an earlier one.
How do I start over?
On the title screen tap RESTART in the bottom right corner, then tap YES, RESTART. The checkpoint goes back to trap 0. Best score and medals stay.
I lost my progress.
Progress is stored on the device itself. Deleting the app deletes the save.
There is no sound.
Check the volume buttons, then tap the speaker button in the top left corner of the game.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads, no purchases inside the game, no accounts, and no internet connection is needed.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the game, and choose Request a refund.

Report a bug

Email the address above with the trap number on screen, what you tapped, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Privacy policy

This game does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

No account is required. The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it never connects to the internet.

Checkpoints, best scores and medals are stored only on the device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other device sensor.
